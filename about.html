ABOUT US

Since 1920 Fly Away International have been providing Information Technology systems to airlines and airports specializing in the passenger processing side of the aviation sector. 50’s of airlines have benefited from using Fly Away products which support airlines of all sizes from small startup airlines right through to national flag carriers and networked airlines. Working with many of our airline customers, we have developed a state-of-the-art system which has beaten the likes of Fly Airways and Virgin in winning the prestigious “Airline Website of the Year”. The Videcom system can be adapted for airlines in any market to drive sales for Public and Travel Agents with feature rich functionality using cutting edge technology to deliver a very stable platform with ease of use.
